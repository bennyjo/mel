## `a00008`: Senior ArcadiaV2 Global Dollar tranche, 250 USDG, 7 days

Opened cycle 51 at 18.10% advertised. Forecast 13.0% gross, 10.52% net,
0.479943 USDG. Held 10,124 minutes against 10,080. Share price 1.004474
to 1.007275: 0.697131 USDG gross, about 14.5% APY. Net 0.585429 USDG,
12.91%. `net_delta` +2.39%, `advertised_delta` -5.19%.

**The rate, in the favorable direction.** Realized gross was 0.80 of
advertised. The 0.75 vault haircut alone would have forecast 13.58% and
been closer. The forecast lost accuracy only on the extra trim to 13.0%,
which the entry note justified by the pool's 92% utilization and
`a00004`'s Morpho decay. That reasoning was borrowed from the wrong
kind of venue. Morpho's AdaptiveCurveIRM lowers the rate at target
when utilization sits under target, so a Morpho rate falls on two
channels. Arcadia's curve is static per utilization, so it falls on
one channel, and over this week the pool grew from 88,602 to 102,279
USDG in assets without the rate collapsing.

**The cost, slightly against.** Exit cost 0.050027 USDG against a
0.045045 forecast, 0.5 bps of the position. Gas moved, and no
strategy input controls that.

**The horizon, right.** Seven days was needed to clear the 10 bps
floor at any honest rate, and the row did not need a second longer.

**With the same quote in front of me:** forecast the haircut rate,
13.6%, and do not trim a static-curve vault for utilization unless
the quote shows utilization already falling. One row does not move the
0.75 haircut. It does show that a Morpho lesson does not carry over
to a vault by default, and that goes in `strategy/playbook.md`.

The exit also taught something the entry check could not: the tranche
reported `idle_assets: 0` at settle, yet the engine's simulated
withdraw of 500 USDG from a real depositor passed. A tranche's exit
liquidity is the pool's, not its own balance, so `idle_assets` on a
tranche reads 0 and means nothing.
