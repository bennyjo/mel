# Recon and go/no-go: Robinhood Chain (4663), 2026-09-08

Operator-owned. Every number below was read from chain 4663 over JSON-RPC on
2026-09-08 and 2026-09-09. Nothing here is quoted from a website, a
documentation page, or another chain. Block numbers are given so each figure
can be re-read, with one caveat that turns out to shape the whole design: the
public endpoint keeps state for about 6,100 blocks, roughly ten minutes, so
most of these reads can no longer be reproduced at the block they were taken
at. See "What the endpoint will not do".

Reference price for the whole memo: ETH = 2,486.67 USDG, from the v3 0.01%
USDG/WETH pool at block 58,063,369. Gas price 0.2357 gwei at block 58,059,305,
so one unit of gas costs 0.000000586 USDG.

## 4. Spread

Advertised rate means the number the venue itself exposes or that its own live
state implies, before any Mel cost. Morpho rates come from calling the
market's own IRM (`borrowRateView`) and multiplying the borrow APY by
utilization and one minus the market fee. Pool rates come from the change in
`feeGrowthGlobal` over a 582-second window, so they are fees actually credited
to liquidity, not volume multiplied by a fee tier. Vault rates come from the
change in `convertToAssets` over a 406-second window. None of these is a
realized return and none is written into a realized field.

Best minus worst across venues that can absorb a 250 USDG allocation: **63.46
percentage points**. Best minus idle: the same, because idle pays zero and
nothing pays negative. Best minus idle among venues deeper than 1M USDG:
**23.51 points**, the fee yield of the 0.01% pool, and that number carries
impermanent-loss risk that this memo does not net out. Best Morpho market
deeper than 1M USDG minus idle: **5.04 points**.

## 5. Decision: **go**

Three numbers carry it. First, a Morpho round trip costs **0.1651 USDG**, which
is **6.6 basis points** of a 250 USDG allocation, because gas on this chain is
0.2357 gwei and ETH is 2,487 USDG. Second, the best venue that can absorb the
full allocation pays **63.46%** against **0.00%** for idle, and even excluding
that one volatile market the deepest 1M-plus venues pay **5.04%** on Morpho and
**23.51%** in fees on the 0.01% pool. Third, the resulting break-even hold is
**9.1 hours** on the best market and **3.5 days** on the deepest pool, both
inside the one-to-seven-day window the test asks about. A 250 USDG allocation
held seven days nets **+115 bps** at 63.46%, **+23 bps** as an LP at 23.51%
before impermanent loss, and **+3 bps** in the deepest Morpho market at 5.04%.
There is a spread here, it is larger than the cost of moving, and the gap
between the confident number and the uncertain one is exactly what the
allocator exists to learn.

Two caveats belong in the same breath. `min_hold_minutes` is 60, and a one-hour
hold loses money at every rate on this chain: the best market earns 0.0181 USDG
in an hour against 0.1651 USDG of cost, a 5.9 bps loss. The floor is not the
decision; the forecast horizon is, and the engine must refuse an hour-long
allocation on cost grounds even though the config permits it. And size is not
neutral. At 50 USDG the same round trip is 33 bps and the one-day hurdle is
120.5% APY, so nothing but the 63% market clears in a week. The agent should
allocate at full size or stay idle.

**The number that would flip this to no-go: gas price.** At 0.2357 gwei the
seven-day Morpho hurdle is 3.44% APY and most of the venue list clears it. A
ten-fold rise to 2.4 gwei puts the round trip at 66 bps, lifts the seven-day
hurdle to 34% APY, and leaves only the 63% market above water, which is the one
market whose own history says it will not stay there. The engine should treat
gas price as a first-class input to every forecast and recheck this memo's
verdict whenever the round trip exceeds 25 bps at full size.

## What the endpoint will not do

Findings that constrain the engine, not just this memo.

- **State is served for about 6,100 blocks, roughly ten minutes.** Older blocks
  return `-32000 metadata is not found, <block>`. This is not an archive node
  and there is no historical state at any depth beyond ten minutes. Settlement
  must therefore read live state at settle time and the ledger must record
  entry state at entry time, because it will be unreadable eleven minutes
  later. A settlement test must replay recorded state, not re-read the chain.
- **The batch limit counts calls, not bytes.** A batch of 60 `eth_call` is
  accepted and 70 is refused, and 100 is refused even when every call is a bare
  `decimals()`. `core/rpc.py` now batches 50.
- **`eth_getLogs` caps results at 10,000 per query.** USDG `Approval` events
  exceed that in 8,000 blocks, and the deepest pool's `Swap` events exceed it in
  20,000 blocks. Windows must be sized by expected match density.
- **Real users on 4663 transact through ERC-4337.** Every recent Morpho supply
  and every recent vault deposit sampled arrived through EntryPoint v0.7 at
  `0x0000000071727de22e5e9d8baf0edac6f37da032`, costing 475,000 gas for the
  whole UserOp. Receipt-derived gas for these actions measures the smart-account
  bundle, not the action, which is why the numbers in table 3 come from
  `eth_estimateGas` wherever a direct caller could be found.
- **Vault share prices are quantized.** Steakhouse USDG carries a 6-decimal
  share price, so over a 406-second window the smallest observable move is one
  part per million, which is 7.7% APY. A trailing rate for a vault needs an hour
  or more of separation to be worth quoting. At `min_hold_minutes` of 60 the
  resolution is 0.88% APY, which is usable but not precise.

Recon cost 354 HTTP requests carrying 3,516 JSON-RPC calls across 31 script
runs, of which 106 requests were throttled and retried. Every run is recorded in
`journal/rpc-usage.jsonl`. A cycle that quotes every confirmed venue and settles
a handful of positions is a few dozen requests, so the public endpoint is
adequate for hourly operation and the constraint that actually bites is the
ten-minute state window, not the rate limit.

## Correction, 2026-09-09: the hold arithmetic above is simple interest

Every break-even hold and net figure in sections 3 to 5 prorates a rate as
`apy * t`. The rates in section 4 are effective annual rates - Morpho's comes
from `expm1` of the market's per-second rate - so the correct prorate is
`(1 + apy) ** t - 1`. `core/cost.py` uses the compounding-consistent form, and
its numbers therefore run below this memo's on any hold longer than a day.

| Figure | As published | Compounding-consistent |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Break-even hold, Morpho `0xaa586d26` at 63.46%, 250 USDG | 9.1 h | 11.8 h |
| Break-even hold, v3 0.01% LP at 23.51%, 250 USDG | 82.9 h | 92.1 h |
| Break-even hold, Morpho `0x1efd13a2` at 5.04%, 250 USDG | 114.8 h | 117.6 h |
| Seven-day net, 63.46% | +115 bps | +88 bps |
| Seven-day net, 23.51% LP | +23 bps | +18 bps |
| Seven-day net, 5.04% | +3 bps | +3 bps |

The go verdict does not move. Every break-even hold stays inside the one-to-
seven-day window the test asked about, and the margin over a 6.6 bps round trip
is still an order of magnitude on the best venue. What changes is that the
engine forecasts about a third less than this memo did on a week-long hold at a
high rate, which is the right direction for a number the agent will be scored
against.

## Correction, 2026-09-09: the vault count in section 1 was a floor

Section 1 records 14 ERC-4626 USDG vaults, which was the 14 deepest of the 40
that the recon's sampled scan reached. Running `core/venues.py discover` over
the last six million blocks - about a week - found **127** USDG vaults with
activity in that window, and `journal/venues.jsonl` now holds 329 rows.

One of them belongs in section 2's depth table and was missing from it:
Steakhouse Turbo USDG (`0xbeefff136e3684273e6aa75a1669b784b373a4fd`), holding
**8,077,244 USDG**, which would rank eighth. It carries no advertised rate: it
has emitted two ERC-4626 events in six million blocks, both for 1 USDG, so
there is nothing to read a share-price change from. That is reported as no
rate, not as zero.

Section 4's vault rates also change, and for a reason that matters more than
the numbers. `convertToAssets` over a seven-minute window resolves no better
than about 4% APY, which is why Steakhouse USDG read 7.72% and Spark Savings
read 0.00% there - both were quantization, not yield. Read from the vaults' own
`Deposit` and `Withdraw` events over a week, the two settle at **3.70%** and
**3.44%**, in line with the deep Morpho markets rather than above and below
them. The go verdict is unaffected; the vault class simply turns out to pay
about what lending pays, which is what it should.
