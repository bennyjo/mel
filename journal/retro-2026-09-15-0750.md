One thing the settlement did show. The row was written for a 3-day horizon
and was held 151.6 hours, about 6.3 days, because no cycle ran between
2026-09-10 06:47 and 2026-09-15 07:49. For an idle row that costs nothing.
For a deployed row it would mean an exit priced five days after the horizon
the forecast was made for, which is a horizon error the strategy did not
choose. `a00004` (Morpho, 7 days) matures 2026-09-17 01:14 UTC; if the loop
is down again then, its settlement will carry the same overrun. Nothing in
`strategy/` can fix that, so it goes in `journal/cycles.md` as a note for the
operator rather than as a strategy edit.
