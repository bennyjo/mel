`net_delta` -3.59%, `advertised_delta` -8.96%, `idle_delta` +8.99%. The
position beat idle and was profitable. It missed its own forecast, and the
miss is entirely on the rate.

**Rate: wrong.** The advertised 17.95% was read at 90.3% utilization, with a
borrow APY of 19.9%. Over the hold the market read 8.75% at 70.9% (cycle 16),
6.80% at 61.6% (cycle 22), 6.96% at 62.9% (cycle 28), 7.26% at 64.8% (cycle
34), and 9.58% at 77.0% at exit, where the borrow APY is 12.4%. Two things
fell at once: utilization, which multiplies the borrow rate into the supply
rate, and the borrow rate itself, which the AdaptiveCurveIRM lowers while
utilization sits under its 90% target. The realized gross is 57% of the
advertised. The 0.85 haircut assumed the rate was a level; it was a peak. The
entry note bet that "mean reversion from a spike" explained the 90.3%
utilization and would keep the rate up. Reversion happened, and it reverted
the rate, not the utilization.

**Cost: wrong the other way, and not a strategy input.** The exit was
forecast at entry gas (0.162 gwei) and paid at 0.050 gwei, so the round trip
cost 0.033 USDG less than forecast. That covered about a quarter of the rate
miss. Nothing in `strategy/` sets the gas forecast, and a forecast that
leans on gas falling is a forecast of luck.

**Horizon: right.** Held 10,080.08 minutes against a 10,080-minute horizon.
The loop was up. Seven days was also the right length: at the realized
10.28% gross, a 3-day hold would have netted about 6 bps, under the floor,
while 7 days netted 16.5 bps, still above the 10 bps `min_expected_net_bps`.
So with the right rate in front of it the strategy would still have opened
this row, at this horizon, and would have been right to.

**With the same quote in front of me:** forecast 10.3% gross, not 15.25%,
which is a 0.57 haircut on the advertised rate. The tell was in the quote:
utilization at or above the IRM target after a two-week swing from 10% to
81% is the top of a cycle, and the adaptive rate has nowhere to go but down
once borrowers repay. `strategy/risk.json` now carries 0.60 for Morpho, the
observed ratio rounded up, and the playbook says why.
