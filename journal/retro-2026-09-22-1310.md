# Retro, 2026-09-22 13:10 UTC (cycle 157)

One row settled: `a00007`, idle, 250 USDG, opened in cycle 16 on
2026-09-15 with a 10,080-minute horizon. Held 168.0 hours, realized
+0.00% against a +0.00% forecast, zero cost.

## Was the forecast wrong about the rate, the cost, or the horizon?

Not the rate and not the cost. Idle earns nothing on this chain and pays
nothing to enter or leave, and both numbers came back exactly as written.

**The horizon.** This is the only one of the three that was a decision,
and it was the wrong one. Idle is the single position whose round trip is
0.0000 USDG at every size, so the only thing a long idle horizon buys is
a lockup: the row holds 250 of the 1,000 USDG bankroll for its whole term
and `core/ledger.py` will not let that capital be considered again until
it settles. A deployed row pays 1.5 to 6.0 bps to exit early, so a long
horizon there is a real commitment bought with a real cost. Idle pays
nothing, so a seven-day idle horizon forfeits seven days of optionality
and receives nothing in exchange.

`a00007` spent its seven days in exactly the situation that argument
describes. The board it was opened against was not the board today: the
vault rate scan was throttled on every full cycle from 2026-09-17 to
2026-09-21, so no vault carried a readable rate, and today it came back
clean with three vaults above 68% advertised. That the three turned out
to be unallocatable is not the point. The point is that the board's
information changed several times inside a horizon chosen when the agent
could not see any of it, and the row could not respond.

**With the same quote in front of me:** open idle at 1,440 minutes, not
10,080. The cost of being wrong about a one-day idle horizon is one more
zero-cost row in the ledger; the cost of being wrong about a seven-day
one is six days of a quarter of the bankroll. That asymmetry does not
depend on what the board is paying, so it is a rule, not a judgment
call, and it goes in `strategy/playbook.md` under rule 6.

This does not change any haircut. `a00004` is still the only settled
deployed row and the 0.60 Morpho haircut still rests on it alone.
